Career & Experience

Mr Satheesh Iype is a Consultant Surgeon in Hepato-Pancreato-Biliary (HPB) Surgery and Liver Transplantation based at the Royal Free Hospital, London, since January 2019. His clinical work focuses on the surgical management of conditions affecting the liver, pancreas, gallbladder, bile duct and spleen, spanning both cancer and benign disease, as well as living donor liver transplantation.

He qualified in Medicine (MBBS) from Medical College, Kottayam, Mahatma Gandhi University, in 1997, before completing postgraduate surgical training in India, including a Masters in Surgery and an MCh in Surgical Gastroenterology. He moved to the UK in 2006, undertaking fellowships at the Royal London Hospital, the Royal Free Hospital, and Cambridge University Hospital, followed by higher surgical training across the East of England deanery. In 2011 he was awarded an MPhil in Transplant Surgery from the University of Cambridge, and holds Fellowships from both the Royal College of Surgeons of England and the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh.

Since joining the Royal Free Hospital, he has taken on clinical leadership for living donor liver transplantation and for novel machine perfusion technologies, having initiated the hospital's normothermic and normothermic regional perfusion programmes. He is an active clinical researcher with more than 60 peer-reviewed publications, and has presented at international meetings including the ILTS Congress, the ESOT Congress, and the IHPBA World Congress.
